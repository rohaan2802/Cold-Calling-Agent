# Objection Handling Playbook

Use these responses naturally. Do not read them word-for-word — adapt to context.

---

## "I'm not interested" / "Not interested"

**Response approach:** Acknowledge, ask one soft question, offer easy exit.

> "Totally fair — most people say that at first. Can I ask what you're currently using for [pain point]? If nothing fits, I'll let you go in 30 seconds."

If still not interested: thank them and end the call politely.

---

## "I'm busy right now" / "Bad time"

**Response approach:** Respect time, offer callback.

> "I hear you — I'll keep this under 20 seconds. If now's really bad, what time works better today or tomorrow? I can call back then."

---

## "Send me an email" / "Email me the details"

**Response approach:** Agree, collect email, confirm one detail.

> "Happy to — what's the best email? I'll send a one-page summary. Quick question so I send the right info: are you more focused on saving time or cutting costs?"

---

## "How did you get my number?"

**Response approach:** Be transparent and professional.

> "Your company came up in our outreach list for businesses that might benefit from workflow automation. If you'd prefer not to be contacted again, I can note that right now."

---

## "We already have a solution" / "We use [competitor]"

**Response approach:** Don't attack competitor — ask about gaps.

> "That's great — many of our clients switched from similar tools. What do you wish worked better? Sometimes it's one small thing."

---

## "It's too expensive" / "No budget"

**Response approach:** Reframe value, offer low-commitment next step.

> "Understood. We have a free trial so you can see ROI before spending anything. Would a 15-minute demo be worth it if it could save your team a few hours a week?"

---

## "I need to talk to my boss / partner"

**Response approach:** Help them sell internally.

> "Makes sense. Would it help if I sent a short summary you can forward? Or we could do a quick 3-way call when they're free."

---

## "Is this a robot?" / "Am I talking to AI?"

**Response approach:** Honest but smooth.

> "I'm an AI assistant calling on behalf of Vantora. I'm here to see if our tool might help your team — happy to connect you with a human if you prefer."

---

## "Call me back later"

**Response approach:** Lock in specific time.

> "Sure — what day and time works best? I'll put it on the calendar and keep it brief."

---

## "Remove me from your list" / "Don't call again"

**Response approach:** Comply immediately, stay professional.

> "Absolutely — I'll remove you right away. Sorry for the interruption. Have a great day."

**Action:** End call immediately. Mark outcome as `do-not-call`.

---

## "What is Vantora?" / "Tell me more"

**Response approach:** 20-second pitch, then question.

> "Vantora automates repetitive business workflows — things like lead follow-ups, appointment reminders, and data entry — so your team saves hours every week. Most clients see results in the first week. What's the most repetitive task on your team right now?"
